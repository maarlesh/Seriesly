Delete Your Account

Last updated: April 5, 2026

You can request deletion of your Seriesly account and all associated data at any time. Once deleted, your watch progress, ratings, and watchlists cannot be recovered.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (email & hashed password)
  • All watch progress stored in Firebase Firestore
  • All ratings and watchlists stored in Firebase Firestore
  • Local app data is removed when you uninstall the app

How to request deletion

1
Send us an email

Email us from the address linked to your Seriesly account so we can verify ownership.

2
Include your account email

Write "Account deletion request" in the subject line and include the email address you registered with.

3
We'll confirm and delete

We will verify the request and permanently delete your account and all associated data within 7 days. You'll receive a confirmation email when done.

⚠  Deletion is permanent and irreversible. All your watch progress, ratings, and watchlists will be lost.
